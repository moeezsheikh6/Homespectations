Description

The Cobb Double Bed is an ultimate space saving design that fits in compact spaces and makes a comfortable room for 2 as well. The fine quality and sturdy craft is well depicted in the design itself. The tufted upholstery on the headboard adds the tiniest bit of detailing to make this design stand out. 

Made to Order. 

Custom Upholstery Available. 

Bed Mattress Sizes:

Queen - Size 1 = 5 feet by 6.5 feet
Queen - Size 2 = 5.5 feet by 6.5 feet
King Size = 6 feet by 6.5 feet

Payment
